Introduction to Module 8
Networking Fundamentals
In this module, you will focus on one of the most important foundational topics in cybersecurity: Computer networking. Understanding how systems and devices are networked together to exchange information is a vital skill for cybersecurity professionals.

The first day of this module will cover the client–server model and network design, and how IP addresses and DNS connect systems.

The second day will cover additional foundational topics such as ports, protocols, and the OSI model. You will then use a networking tool called Wireshark to visualize and analyze networking concepts.

The third day will dive deeper into OSI layers 2, 3, and 4. You will learn tools and protocols related to each layer and perform a SYN scan to search for open ports on a network.

What to Be Aware Of
On Day 1, during the Network Devices activity, you will use a web-based tool called draw.io to design a network.

On Day 2, you will cover the OSI model. It can be difficult to grasp at first, so don't be discouraged if you don't understand it immediately. You may need to review the OSI layers a few times to understand fully.
