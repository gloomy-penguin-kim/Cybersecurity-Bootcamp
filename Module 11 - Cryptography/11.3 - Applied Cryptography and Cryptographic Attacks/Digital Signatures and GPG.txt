Digital Signatures:

To Sign and Encrypt

gpg --output signed_document.sig --sign input_file

To verify a signature and decrypt:

gpg --output unencrypted_output --decrypt signed_document.sig

CLEARSIGN DOCUMENTS CREATION

gpg --output doc.sig  --clearsign doc

CLEARSIGN DOCUMENTS VERIFICATION

gpg --verify doc.sig

DETACHED SIGNATURE CREATION

gpg --output doc.sig --detach-sig doc

DETACHED SIGNATURE VERIFICATION

gpg --verify doc.sig doc
