10.2 Lesson 2: Hardening System Configurations
Overview

On Day 2, you will continue to harden BSC's Linux server. This day's focus is on BSC’s Linux server's system settings such as SSH, system packages, services, and logging configurations.
Project Objectives

By the end of today's class, you will:

    Audit and secure the organization's SSH settings.
    Review and update system packages.
    Disable any unnecessary services.
    Enable and configure logging.
