Introduction

Welcome to the ML/AI in Cybersecurity module, part of our comprehensive Cybersecurity Boot Camp. This module is designed to equip you with essential knowledge and practical skills in applying artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) in the realm of cybersecurity. The module is divided into three detailed lessons focusing on different aspects of ML/AI in cybersecurity: general concepts, defense mechanisms, and offensive strategies.
Lesson Plan Summaries

ML/AI in Cybersecurity: General Concepts In the first day, you will be introduced to the fundamentals of AI and ML. The lesson covers the basic definitions, differences between AI and traditional programming, types of AI (narrow AI vs. general AI), and the critical role of AI/ML in enhancing cybersecurity. Ethical considerations and bias in AI technologies are also discussed to ensure you're aware of the broader implications of using AI tools like ChatGPT and WhiteRabbitNeo in cybersecurity applications.

ML/AI in Cybersecurity Defense The second day focuses on how AI and ML are leveraged for cybersecurity defense. You will explore various AI/ML techniques for threat detection and prevention and gain hands-on experience with AI tools designed for cybersecurity defense. This session aims to enhance your understanding of AI's role in protecting digital assets and infrastructure from potential threats.

ML/AI in Cybersecurity Offense In the final day, the focus shifts to the offensive capabilities of AI and ML in cybersecurity. This includes understanding how these technologies are used to identify vulnerabilities in systems and devise strategies to exploit them effectively. The lesson provides practical insights into AI/ML techniques for offensive security, complemented by hands-on experiences with relevant AI tools.
