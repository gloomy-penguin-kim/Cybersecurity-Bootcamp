Lesson 2: ML/AI in Cybersecurity Defense
Overview

Today's class will provide you with an overview of AI/ML as it is used for defense purposes in cybersecurity. You will learn how AI and ML are used for threat detection and prevention and use AI tools for cybersecurity defense.
What You’ll Learn

By the end of the class, you will have covered the following concepts:

    Understand how AI is used in cybersecurity defense.
    Learn about specific AI/ML techniques for threat detection and prevention.
    Gain hands-on experience with AI tools used in cybersecurity defense.
