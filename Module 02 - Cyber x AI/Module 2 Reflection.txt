Module 2 Reflection

Congratulations on completing the ML/AI in Cybersecurity module! Through this module, you've been introduced to a variety of concepts and practical applications that highlight the role of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) in both defending and attacking within the cybersecurity landscape. Here’s what you have accomplished:

    Understood foundational concepts of AI and ML, distinguishing between AI and traditional programming methods and between different types of AI applications in cybersecurity.
    Explored AI and ML in defense, learning about their capabilities in threat detection and the various tools that apply these technologies to protect systems.
    Investigated the offensive use of AI and ML, where you examined how these technologies are employed to identify and exploit system vulnerabilities.

Reflection Questions

        Identify any areas where you feel more knowledge is needed or where you have specific concerns about the application of AI and ML.
