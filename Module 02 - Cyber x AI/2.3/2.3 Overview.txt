Lesson 3: ML/AI in Cybersecurity Offense
Overview

In today's class, you will dive into the critical role that Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) play in cybersecurity offense. These advanced technologies are transforming the way we identify and address vulnerabilities in systems, making our defenses stronger and more proactive.
What You’ll Learn

By the end of the class, you will understand the concepts of:

    Understanding how AI is used in cybersecurity offense.
    Learn about specific AI/ML techniques for offensive security.
    Gain hands-on experience with AI tools used in cybersecurity offense
