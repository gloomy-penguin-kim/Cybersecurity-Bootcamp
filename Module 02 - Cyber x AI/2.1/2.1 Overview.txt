Lesson 1: ML/AI in Cybersecurity
Overview

Today's class will provide you with an overview of artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning (ML) concepts, their differences, and their importance in cybersecurity. We will also emphasize ethical considerations and bias in AI.
What You’ll Learn

By the end of the class, you will have covered the following concepts:

    Definition of AI and ML.
    Differences between AI and traditional programming.
    Types of AI (narrow AI vs. general AI).
    Importance of AI/ML in cybersecurity.
    Ethics and bias in AI.
    The use of ChatGPT and similar tools in cybersecurity.
